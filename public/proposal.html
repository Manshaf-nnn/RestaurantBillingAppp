Why it matters

Free your team from taking orders — so they can focus on hospitality.

On a busy night, orders get missed, handwriting gets misread, and guests wait just to flag someone down. TableFlow removes that bottleneck completely: guests order themselves, the kitchen sees it the moment it's placed, and your staff spend their time serving and delighting — not ferrying slips of paper.

up to 75% fewer order-taking staff needed

Because your guests browse, order and pay right from their table, you no longer need a large team simply to take and run orders. How it works

From seated to served — in four simple steps.

It feels effortless for your guests, and it runs itself for you.

1

Scan & sit

The guest scans the QR on the table and enters their table number. The menu opens instantly — no app, no sign-up.

2

Browse & order

They explore your menu with photos, prices and dietary tags, add items, leave notes like “extra spicy”, and place the order.

3

Kitchen cooks

The order lands on the kitchen screen the same second — with the table number in bold — so the line knows exactly what and where.

4

Track & pay

The guest watches “preparing → ready” on their phone, then pays at the table by QR, card or cash. Table turns faster.
